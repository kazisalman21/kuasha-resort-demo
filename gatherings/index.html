Holud nights and small weddings

The lawn by the lake takes 150 seated under lanterns; the Long Veranda takes 80 for dinner. Book the whole estate (22 rooms, about 50 guests) and the place is yours.

Two or three generations, one long table. We plan the days around the oldest and youngest guests, and the cooks will make Nani’s recipe if you bring it.

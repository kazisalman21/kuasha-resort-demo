    1. A reservations host confirms availability by your chosen channel within the hour (08:00–22:00).
    2. A 50% advance by bKash, Nagad, card or bank transfer secures the stay. The balance is paid on arrival.
    3. You receive a confirmation voucher and a driver’s note with the final turns.

    Free up to 7 days before arrival; the advance is refunded within 10 working days. Within 7 days, the advance can be moved to new dates once within six months.

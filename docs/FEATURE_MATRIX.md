## 3. Decisions for the Kuasha concept

| Feature | What the research shows | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Availability search with nightly prices** | Present at Soneva and Capella; absent at every BD site | **Build (simulated)** | You chose this. The calendar shows nightly prices and simulated sold-out nights, labelled "demo". It ends in a *request* that staff confirm, which is how BD resorts actually operate. |
| Date + guest bar on homepage | Soneva, Six Senses, Gleneagles (desktop and mobile), Brando | **Build**: docked to the bottom of the hero on desktop; on mobile, one "Check dates" button that opens a bottom sheet | Gleneagles' 3-field mobile hero is cramped. Brando's bottom sheet is the best mobile booking UI we saw. |
| Persistent mobile action | Rosewood, Amanpuri, Capella, Brando, Post Ranch; WhatsApp floats on 5 BD sites | **Build**: one bottom bar with *Call · WhatsApp · Check dates*, shown after the hero | Replaces separate floating WhatsApp, phone FAB and offer tabs. That clutter was a recurring BD weakness. |
| Desktop sticky booking bar | Four Seasons only (after scroll) | **Do not build** | The header "Check availability" button is always visible. A second bar takes space from the photography. |
| WhatsApp | NIHI (top-tier), Soneva footer, 6 BD sites | **Build** (pre-filled message containing the stay details) | Local behaviour, and validated even at luxury level. |
| Messenger | 3 BD sites | **Footer link only** | Lower priority than WhatsApp and the phone. |
| Phone in header (desktop) | Most BD sites; few international | **Build** as a quiet text link | BD guests call. Large phone numbers as display type (Novem) look cheap. |
| Prices "from ৳" with VAT clarity | Novem, Paragon, Mermaid; "++ / +++" notation at Chuti and Turag | **Build**, stating "inclusive of 10% service charge and 15% VAT" | Local guests expect to know; "+++" is opaque. |
| Room facts + floor plan | Soneva, Gleneagles, Cap Juluca, Capella, Post Ranch | **Build** facts; floor plan as a simple drawn SVG | Facts build trust. The floor plan is cheap and makes the room page feel premium. |
| 360° tour | Capella | **Later** | Needs the real property. |
| Offers with terms | Gleneagles, Soneva, Grand Sultan | **Build** (3 offers: nights rule, validity, inclusions, terms) | Concrete terms read as trustworthy. |
| Day-long package | Gazipur sites (Namir, Kazi) | **Build as one offer** | Sreemangal is about 4 h from Dhaka, so day-outs matter less than near-Dhaka. It still serves Sylhet and Moulvibazar locals. |
| Experiences with duration and price | Four Seasons, Six Senses, Fogo | **Build** | Decision data is the strongest pattern for this section. |
| Weddings / holud / corporate retreat enquiry | Almost universal; strong local demand | **Build** a "Gatherings" page with capacity facts and a structured enquiry | |
| Getting here with times | Aman, Oberoi, Cap Juluca; Dhaka distance on 6 BD sites | **Build**: road, train and air with honest ranges | Arrival logistics are the main worry for a remote stay. |
| Station / airport pickup | Sampan (airport pick & drop) | **Build** as a booking add-on | Sreemangal railway station is the natural arrival point. |
| Driver accommodation | Sampan ("Affordable Driver's Accommodation") | **Build** as an FAQ answer and booking option | A very local need: families often travel with a hired driver. |
| Policies (check-in, ID, child pricing, cancellation, advance) | Bhawal, Novem | **Build** in "Visit" plus the booking summary | |
| FAQ | Namir | **Build** (8 real questions) | |
| Guest reviews | Grand Sultan, Namir (the widget showed 1-star reviews) | **Placeholder slot only, clearly marked** | Your brief forbids fake reviews. The live Google widget at Namir shows the risk of an unmoderated feed. Later: 2–3 curated, attributed, dated reviews linked to source. |
| Awards / press | Many international sites | **None** (no fake awards) | Slot documented for a real client. |
| Newsletter | Several international | **Do not build** | Low value for a single BD resort; Facebook/Instagram follow serves this. |
| Gift vouchers | Gleneagles, Capella, Fogo, Ett Hem | **Later** | Needs a payment back-end. |
| Language: Bangla | None of 19 BD sites | **Not in v1**; architecture ready | English is the norm for premium BD resort sites. A Bangla version is a strong phase-2 differentiator, so it is costed in the client summary. |
| Currency selector | Four Seasons, Soneva | **Do not build** | BDT-first market; foreign guests can read an approximate USD note. |
| bKash / Nagad advance payment | Novem, Namir | **Show as next step after the request**, no live payment | Matches the "50% advance confirms" practice. A live gateway needs merchant accounts. |
| Local time + weather | Six Senses, Oberoi | **Build** local time + a "this season" note (no live weather API) | A live weather API is overbuild; a season note is more useful. |
| Hero video | 12 of 14 international | **Photo hero** (video-ready component with poster fallback) | Many video heroes failed to render in our capture, and the demo has no real footage. |
| Accessibility | Only a third-party widget on Rosewood | **Build accessibility in, no overlay widget** | Semantic HTML, focus states, reduced motion, contrast. |
| Structured data | 7 of 12 BD sites | **Build** `Resort` / `LodgingBusiness` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` | |
| Cookie consent | All international | **Not in demo** (no tracking scripts) | Add with analytics on a real launch. |

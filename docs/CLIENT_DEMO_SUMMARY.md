# Kuasha — what a website like this does for a resort

*A plain-language summary to share with resort owners. "Kuasha" is a sample resort used to demonstrate the work; your resort's own name, photographs, rooms and prices would replace it.*

## Why a proper website matters now

- Most guests now find a resort on their phone, through Facebook, Instagram or Google, then look for a website to check whether the place is real, what it costs and how to book.
- When there's no website, or a weak one, they book through a booking site that takes a commission, or they go somewhere else.
- We looked at 19 Bangladeshi resort and hotel websites. Most had at least one visible problem: broken hero videos, copy from templates about other countries, spelling mistakes, prices that didn't say whether VAT was included, or no clear way to book on a phone. A resort with a calm, clear and correct website stands out immediately.

## What guests get

1. **A first impression that matches the place.** Large photographs, unhurried writing and clear facts: how many rooms, how far from Dhaka, what's included.
2. **Rooms explained properly.** Size, beds, how many people fit, the view, what's included, check-in times and a simple floor plan. A comparison table helps families choose.
3. **Prices they can trust.** Rates in taka, with service charge and VAT stated, and offers with exact dates and terms. No surprises at the desk.
4. **Booking the way Bangladeshi guests actually book.** Guests pick dates on a calendar that shows prices, choose a room, add a station pickup or a room for their driver, and send a request. Your team confirms by phone or WhatsApp and takes a 50% advance by bKash, Nagad, card or bank, exactly as most resorts already work. Guests who prefer to talk can call or WhatsApp from every page, with their dates already written into the message.
5. **Answers before they ask.** Getting here by road, train or air; the best season; policies on children, IDs and cancellation; and a question-and-answer section.
6. **Events made easy.** Holud nights, weddings, family reunions and company retreats each get a page with capacities and a short enquiry form.

## Built for phones first

On a phone the site is laid out differently, not just shrunk: one strong photograph and two buttons on the first screen, a "Call · WhatsApp · Check dates" bar that stays at the bottom, swipeable rooms, and a booking flow with large buttons and a running total. Pages are light (about 1 MB to open the home page) so they load quickly on mobile data.

## What it does for the business

- **More direct bookings**, so less commission paid to booking sites.
- **Fewer repetitive calls**, because prices, policies and directions are already answered.
- **Better enquiries**, since every request arrives with dates, guests, room and extras filled in.
- **A premium image** that supports higher rates and corporate and wedding business.
- **Seasonal offers** (winter weekdays, family stays, day visits) that fill quiet periods.

## Found on Google

Each page is written and structured for search: clear titles and descriptions, proper headings, fast loading, image descriptions, and the hidden "structured data" Google reads for hotels, rooms, FAQs and breadcrumbs. Share previews look good when a link is posted on Facebook or WhatsApp.

## What we would need from you

- **A one-day professional photo shoot** of your property (we provide the shot list). This matters more than anything else.
- Your room types, rates by season, policies and current offers.
- Phone and WhatsApp numbers for reservations and events, and your Facebook and Instagram links.
- A decision on booking: keep "request and confirm" (works from day one), or connect an online booking engine if you already use one.

## Easy to grow later

The site is built so that new pages, offers and seasons are quick to add. Options for later:
- A **Bangla version** of the whole site (no competitor we checked has one).
- **Online advance payment** by bKash, Nagad or card (SSLCommerz or similar).
- A **live booking engine** connected to your room inventory.
- A gift-voucher page, a blog for local travel guides, and verified Google reviews.

## About this demonstration

Kuasha is fictional. Its rooms, prices, offers and contact details are samples, and the photographs come from a free photo library, so they don't show any one real resort. The calendar's availability is simulated, and buttons that would call or message show a notice instead. For your resort, all of this is replaced with your real details and photographs before anything goes live.
